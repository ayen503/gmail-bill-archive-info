Gmail Bill Archive

Gmail Bill Archive is a personal desktop utility that downloads credit-card statement PDF attachments from an authorized Gmail account and saves the original files in a folder selected by the account owner, including an Obsidian vault.

The utility runs on the owner's Mac. It identifies statement emails using bank sender addresses and monthly statement subjects, preserves PDF passwords and encryption, and skips files already archived. Emails without a statement attachment are reported for manual handling. The utility does not automatically sign in to bank websites.

Gmail access is read-only. The utility does not send messages, delete messages, or change mailbox labels or read status. It is intended for the owner's personal use and is not a public mailbox-processing service.

For details about access, storage, retention, and revocation, see the Privacy Policy page on this site.
